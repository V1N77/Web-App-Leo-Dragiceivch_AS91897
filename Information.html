Sleep Information

Getting enough quality sleep is an important part of maintaining a healthy lifestyle. Below are some recommendations that can help improve your sleep routine and make it easier to fall asleep.

The 10-3-2-1-0 Sleep Rule

The 10-3-2-1-0 rule is a simple way to organise your routine before going to sleep. Each number represents a recommended time period before bed.

10

10 Hours Before Bed

Avoid caffeine during the ten hours before going to sleep.

3

3 Hours Before Bed

Avoid alcohol and eating during the three hours before going to sleep.

2

2 Hours Before Bed

Finish work or other work-related activities around two hours before going to bed.

1

1 Hour Before Bed

Avoid using screens during the hour before sleeping.

0

0 Snoozes

Try to avoid repeatedly pressing the snooze button when your alarm goes off.

Other Sleep Recommendations

Keep a Consistent Schedule

Try to go to sleep and wake up around the same time each day to maintain a regular sleep routine.

Avoid Nicotine in the Evening

Avoid using nicotine too far into the evening as it can interfere with your sleep.

Exercise During the Day

Regular physical activity during the day can support healthier sleep.

Limit Screens After Waking

Consider avoiding screens for around 30–60 minutes after waking up. This can help reduce immediate screen use in the morning.

Personalised Recommendation

Your sleep routine can be influenced by your personal circumstances and daily routine. Use the Personal tab to provide information that can help make your recommendations more relevant to you.

Having Sleep Concerns?

If you experience ongoing or concerning problems with your sleep, it is recommended that you speak with a doctor or another qualified health professional.
